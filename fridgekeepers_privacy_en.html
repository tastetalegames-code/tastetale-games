Privacy Policy

Game: Fridgekeepers: Night Shift
Developer: TasteTale Games
Effective date: 15 November 2026
Contact: tastetalegames@gmail.com

This policy describes how the Android game Fridgekeepers: Night Shift handles data. The game is a single-player, offline puzzle experience. It does not require players to create an account or sign in.

Information stored on your device

To keep your place in the game, Fridgekeepers saves information locally on your device, including unlocked and completed levels, star ratings, attempts and best results. It also stores preferences such as music, sound effects and reduced-motion settings. This information is used only for game progress and preferences; it is not sent to TasteTale Games by the reviewed game build.

Data collection, sharing and advertising

The reviewed game source and Android test build contain no advertising or analytics SDK integration and do not request the Android Internet permission. Fridgekeepers does not transmit gameplay records or personal information to TasteTale Games or advertising and analytics providers in that build. We do not sell player information.

Google Play and device services

Google Play and your Android device may separately process installation, diagnostics, backup and other platform data under their own privacy policies. Such processing is distinct from data transmission by the game itself. Device backup or restore settings may retain copies of locally stored game progress.

Support emails

If you choose to email tastetalegames@gmail.com, we may receive the address, message and any information you voluntarily provide. We use these messages to respond to support requests; this information is not obtained from gameplay. You can contact us to request deletion of a support message, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To delete the locally stored data, use Android Settings → Apps → Fridgekeepers → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Fridgekeepers. Backups managed by the operating system may require separate deletion under your device or account settings.

Children

Fridgekeepers does not request personal information or registration in the game. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions about privacy.

Changes

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. Any revision will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email tastetalegames@gmail.com.
