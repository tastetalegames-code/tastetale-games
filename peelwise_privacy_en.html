PEELWISE — Privacy Policy

Game: PEELWISE
Developer: TasteTale Games
Effective date: 15 November 2026
Version described: Android 1.0.0
Contact: tastetalegames@gmail.com

Overview

PEELWISE is a single-player, offline game about carefully peeling vegetables and minimizing food waste in a futuristic colony kitchen. You do not need to register an account or sign in to play. This policy explains how the Android version 1.0.0 handles information.

Information stored on your device

PEELWISE stores game information locally on your device to remember your progress and preferences. This may include unlocked or completed shifts and levels, scores, stars, attempts, food saved and waste statistics, records from The Endless Shift, and audio or gameplay settings. These records are used to resume gameplay and display your results. They are not uploaded to a TasteTale Games account or game server by the described release.

Data collection, sharing and advertising

The described release does not require an account and does not contain in-game advertising or third-party gameplay analytics features. PEELWISE does not ask players to provide names, email addresses, contacts, precise location or payment information for gameplay. We do not sell player information or share gameplay records with advertising providers.

Google Play and Android services

Google Play, Android and device manufacturers may process installation, security, diagnostic or backup information separately under their own privacy terms. Such platform processing is distinct from data collected by PEELWISE itself. If device backup is enabled, local game data may be backed up or restored according to your device and account settings.

Support communications

If you voluntarily contact tastetalegames@gmail.com, we may receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your enquiry or support request. This information is supplied through your email service, not collected during gameplay. You can request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved game progress, statistics and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To remove local data, open Android Settings → Apps → PEELWISE → Storage → Clear storage (wording may vary by device), or uninstall PEELWISE. Copies retained by optional operating-system backup services may need to be managed separately in your device or account settings. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us by email.

Children and families

PEELWISE does not require children or other players to submit personal information or create an account to play. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if PEELWISE's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email tastetalegames@gmail.com.
