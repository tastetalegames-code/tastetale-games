Toast Disaster — Privacy Policy

Developer: TasteTale Games
Effective date: 7 November 2026
Contact: tastetalegames@gmail.com

Overview

Toast Disaster is an offline single-player physics puzzle game. This policy describes the behavior of the reviewed release (version 0.7.0). We do not require an account to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves level completion progress, your sound preference, and Service Records statistics such as attempts, first successful completion, recent success and best results in local app storage on your device. These records are used to continue your progress and display your gameplay history.

Data collection, sharing and network access

In the reviewed release, the game does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not request the Android Internet permission, and does not transmit gameplay records or personal data to TasteTale Games or third-party servers. We do not sell player data.

Google Play and your Android operating system may separately process information about installations, purchases, diagnostics or device backups under their own policies. That activity is not the game's own data transmission.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay progress and statistics remain in the app's local storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Device backup or restore systems, if enabled, may separately retain copies subject to your device settings and provider policies. To remove locally saved gameplay history, use your device's Settings → Apps → Toast Disaster → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Children

Toast Disaster does not request account registration or directly collect personal information from players in the reviewed release. If our audience, features or data practices change, we will update this policy and any required notices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new functionality. We will change the effective date above and make the revised policy accessible at this page and through the game.

Contact
